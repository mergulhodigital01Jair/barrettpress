Privacy Policy

Effective August 5, 2026

This policy explains what information Barrett Press collects when you visit this website or purchase a book, how that information is used, and the choices you have. Barrett Press is an independent digital publisher operated from Brazil, selling to customers worldwide.

Who we are

Barrett Press publishes digital books under the author name J. Barrett. You can reach us at support@barrettpress.com for any question about this policy or about your personal information.

Information we collect

  • Purchase information. When you buy a book, the transaction is processed by Hotmart, our payment and delivery platform. Hotmart collects your name, email address, billing details, and payment information. We receive your name, email address, and order details. We never receive or store your full card number.
  • Website usage information. When you visit this site, standard technical data may be recorded, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, approximate location, pages viewed, and the site or advertisement that referred you.
  • Messages you send us. If you email support, we keep your message and our reply so we can help you and keep a record of the request.

Cookies and advertising technologies

This site uses cookies and similar technologies, including the Meta Pixel provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. These tools help us understand how visitors find and use the site, measure whether our advertising works, and show relevant ads on Facebook and Instagram.

You can control cookies through your browser settings, and you can adjust how your data is used for advertising in your Facebook and Instagram ad preferences. Blocking cookies will not prevent you from reading the site or completing a purchase.

How we use your information

  • To deliver the book you purchased and provide access to it
  • To answer support requests and process refund requests
  • To measure and improve our advertising and our website
  • To detect and prevent fraud or misuse
  • To meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

Who your information is shared with

We share information only with the service providers needed to run this business:

  • Hotmart — payment processing, product delivery, customer accounts, and refunds
  • Meta Platforms — advertising delivery and measurement
  • Our website host — serving the pages of this site

Each of these providers has its own privacy policy governing the data it holds. We may also disclose information where required by law.

International transfers

Because our providers operate in several countries, your information may be processed outside your country of residence, including in Brazil, the United States, and the European Union. We rely on our providers' contractual safeguards for these transfers.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for as long as needed to support your purchase and to meet tax and legal requirements. Support emails are kept while they remain useful for helping you. Advertising and analytics data are retained according to the retention periods of the providers named above.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to your personal information, to ask for a correction, to ask for deletion, to object to certain processing, or to receive a copy of your data. Residents of the European Union and United Kingdom hold these rights under the GDPR; residents of California hold comparable rights under the CCPA, including the right not to be discriminated against for exercising them; residents of Brazil hold these rights under the LGPD.

To make a request, email support@barrettpress.com. We will respond within the time required by the law that applies to you. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request.

Children

These books and this website are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Payments are handled by Hotmart over encrypted connections. This website is served over HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work only with established providers and keep the data we hold to a minimum.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Questions about your data? Write to support@barrettpress.com and we will answer.
