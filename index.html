About the press

Barrett Press publishes practical, non-clinical guides written by J. Barrett. Every title is built on the same idea: most people do not need to try harder. They need a smaller first step and a way back in after a bad week.

The books are short on purpose. They are written to be usable on a low-energy day, not admired on a shelf.
